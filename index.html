Questions, answered.

How do payments work?

Card payments are processed by Stripe. The recipient's share is paid out to their connected Stripe account after the platform fee.

What is a milestone agreement?

You pay up front and the funds are held. When you approve the work they are released to the recipient. If the recipient declines, you are refunded.

Why must recipients set up payouts?

Stripe verifies every recipient before they can receive money. It takes a few minutes and is done once.

What does it cost?

A monthly plan, plus a platform fee that is shown before you pay.
